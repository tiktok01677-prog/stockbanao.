Privacy policy

How StockBanao handles product data.

Last updated: November 10, 2026

StockBanao is designed as a local-first product and stock ledger. Please read this policy and update the publisher information before distributing the app.

Information you enter

You may enter product names, codes (SKU), categories, opening quantities, prices, minimum stock alerts, dates, stock movements, business name and notes.

Where it is stored

In the supplied version, these records are saved in your device's browser local storage (or Android WebView storage). No account, database sync or automatic upload of stock records to the developer is implemented.

How data is used

The information you enter is used to calculate stock balances, product values, low-stock alerts, movement history and reports. No payment processing, advertising SDK or analytics SDK is included in this source package.

Hosting and technical logs

When using the hosted website, the web hosting provider (such as GitHub Pages) may process basic access information, such as IP address, browser details and request logs according to its own policies.

Exports and backups

JSON backups and CSV exports are initiated by you. Exported files can contain sensitive business information. Anyone with a copy of a backup may be able to read its contents. Protect backups and share them carefully.

Data retention and deletion

Data remains in local storage until you delete records through the application, clear browser or WebView storage, or remove the application and its storage. Downloaded files need to be deleted separately. See our data deletion instructions.

Security

StockBanao does not encrypt local records with a separate password. Protect your device and keep backups secure. Avoid entering information you do not need for stock management.

Children

StockBanao is intended for business inventory management and is not designed for children.

Policy changes

Future versions may add features or integrations. This policy should be updated when data practices change.
