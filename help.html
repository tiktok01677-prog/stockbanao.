Help & guide

Learn to track products, stock movement and backups.

1. Add a product

Open Products → Add product. Enter the product name, optional SKU and category, a minimum stock alert, purchase price, selling price and opening quantity. Save it.

2. Record incoming stock

Choose Stock In for a product. Enter how many units you received, the date and an optional note. Saving increases its available quantity.

3. Record outgoing stock

Choose Stock Out for a product. Enter a positive integer quantity. StockBanao prevents an outgoing entry that would make the calculated stock negative.

4. Check low stock

Products with available units less than or equal to the chosen minimum appear in Low-stock alerts. An item with 0 units is marked out of stock.

5. Edit or delete records

You can edit products and individual stock entries. Editing or deleting is rejected if the resulting stock balance would become negative. Deleting a product also removes its stock history after you confirm.

6. Export and restore

Open Settings to download a JSON backup. Keep it somewhere safe. You can restore it later, but restoring replaces current app data. CSV exports are for spreadsheets, not for restoring.

7. Where is my data?

Your records are stored locally on the device in the browser or WebView storage. If the browser data is cleared or the app is uninstalled, your records may be lost. Use JSON backups regularly.

8. GitHub Pages & Android WebView

Publish the website over HTTPS using GitHub Pages and put its URL in your Android Studio Java WebView. In some Android WebViews, file download/upload requires additional native Java handling. Test backup/restore before relying on it.
