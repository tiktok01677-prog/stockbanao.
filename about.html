About StockBanao

Practical inventory management for everyday shop owners.

Built for simple stock management

StockBanao helps shops track products, purchase and selling prices, stock received, stock removed, and low-stock alerts.

What the application can do

  • Add and edit products with SKU, category, prices and opening stock.
  • Record stock-in and stock-out quantities, dates and notes.
  • See the quantity and cost value of inventory.
  • Export inventory and activity as CSV or save a JSON backup.

Local-first design

Your inventory entries are stored in your device's browser storage (or the Android WebView app storage). They are not automatically synchronised across devices.

Important limitation

StockBanao is a stock tracking tool, not a point-of-sale system, accounting package or tax-compliant financial report. Stock-out entries reduce quantities, but they are not recorded as sales revenue.
