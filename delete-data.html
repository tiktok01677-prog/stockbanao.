Delete your data

Control and erase locally saved StockBanao records.

Delete individual products and stock entries

Open a product and select Delete product, or delete a specific stock movement from the History screen. Product deletion also deletes its related movements, after confirmation.

Delete all app records

  1. Open StockBanao and go to Settings.
  2. If needed, tap Download backup first.
  3. Tap Delete all records and confirm.

This removes locally saved inventory products and movement history, but leaves app settings in place.

Clear all local app data

Use your browser's site data controls to clear storage for the hosted StockBanao domain. For an Android WebView wrapper, clearing the app's storage through Android settings or uninstalling may remove the stored data. Procedures vary by device.

Previously exported backups

JSON and CSV files that you downloaded are not automatically deleted. Find and delete them from your device or cloud storage separately.

No developer-held stock ledger

This supplied version has no cloud database of user inventory records to delete. Website hosts may keep technical request logs according to their independent policies.
